The pattern

Difficulty is driven far more by the day than the month. Days in the right-hand column (7, 14, 21, 28) and the bottom stub (29–31) sit against edges the board already has, so removing them barely changes the shape you must tile. The 6th is the worst case — interior, directly under the month block, and it strands the cells around it.

Average by day of month

Average by month

The board: twelve months in two rows of six, days 1–31 in rows of seven. Eight pieces — one 2×3 rectangle plus the pentominoes L N P U V Y Z — cover 41 of the 43 cells, leaving the month and the day showing. Every solution here came from exhaustive search, so each list is complete. The board carries 31 days under every month, so it can also express Feb 30 and Apr 31; those six non-dates are excluded from the counts and rankings, but Feb 29 is included. Piece colours match a KUTA-branded copy of this puzzle. The two yellows and the two reds are close on the real blocks, so they are separated slightly here; the piece-letter toggle disambiguates them either way.
